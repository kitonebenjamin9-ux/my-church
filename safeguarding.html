Safeguarding & Child Protection

Our commitment to a safe church environment.

Healing Tabernacle Ministries is committed to treating children, young people and vulnerable people with dignity, care and respect.

Ministry leaders and volunteers should follow the church's safeguarding procedures and report concerns through the appropriate pastoral or safeguarding leadership channel.

Do not use public website forms to report an immediate danger. Contact the appropriate emergency or safeguarding authority when urgent action is required.
