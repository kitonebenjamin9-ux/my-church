A Story of Faith Since 2003

From Twelve Families to a Growing Church Family

Healing Tabernacle started in 2003 as a small prayer gathering of twelve families in a rented hall. Today we're a growing church family committed to seeing broken lives made whole through the power of the Gospel.

From weekly worship services to community outreach, youth mentorship and prayer ministry, everything we do points back to one truth — healing is available to everyone who calls on the name of Jesus.

Our Spiritual Leadership

Bishop Richard and Evelyne Kabenge

Bishop Richard and Evelyne Kabenge have led Healing Tabernacle Ministries for over 23 years, shepherding the congregation with a message of restoration, hope and a radical Apostolic and Prophetic mandate.

Their heart is to turn believers into strong and bold missionaries of our Lord Jesus Christ — grounded in Scripture, strengthened through prayer and equipped to carry the Gospel into their homes, communities and nations.

They believe every person who walks through our doors carries a purpose worth fighting for. Through teaching, mentoring, prayer and pastoral care, they encourage people to discover that purpose and serve God with courage.

Our leadership commitment
To lead with prayer, integrity, compassion and faithfulness to the Word of God — raising disciples who make disciples.

What We Believe

Our Beliefs

Our faith is rooted in the Scriptures and centred on the person and work of Jesus Christ.

The Bible

The Scriptures, both Old and New Testaments, are the inspired Word of God, without error in the original writings. They reveal His will for salvation and are the divine and final authority for Christian faith and life.

God

There is one God, Creator of all things, infinitely perfect and eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ is true God and true man, conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the Virgin Mary. He died on the cross as the complete and final sacrifice for our sins, rose bodily from the dead, ascended into heaven and now serves as our High Priest and Advocate at the right hand of the Father.

The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit glorifies the Lord Jesus Christ. He convicts people of sin, brings new life to those who believe, indwells and guides believers, teaches them and empowers them for godly living and service.

Humanity

Humanity was created in the image of God but fell into sin and is therefore lost. Salvation and spiritual life are received through the new birth by the Holy Spirit.

Salvation

The shed blood of Jesus Christ and His resurrection provide the only basis for justification and salvation. Everyone who receives Jesus Christ by faith is born of the Holy Spirit and becomes a child of God.

The Return of Jesus Christ

We look for the personal, pre-millennial and imminent return of our Lord Jesus Christ. This hope shapes the believer's life, faithfulness and service.

Future Events

There will be a bodily resurrection of all the dead: believers to everlasting blessedness and joy with the Lord, and unbelievers to judgment and everlasting conscious punishment.

The Church

The true Church is made up of all people who have been saved through faith in Jesus Christ, regenerated by the Holy Spirit and united in the body of Christ, of which He is the head.

Baptism, the Holy Spirit & Communion

Water baptism by immersion, following faith in Christ, testifies to death to sin and resurrection to new life. The Lord's Supper is a sacred memorial of Jesus Christ's death, shared by believers in remembrance of Him. We also believe in the ministry and empowering work of the Holy Spirit in the life of the Church.
