ABOUT THE CHURCH

Who we are in Rukungiri

Healing Tabernacle Ministries — Rukungiri Branch is part of one church family with a local heart for the people of Rukungiri and surrounding communities.

OUR HEART

Faith. Family. Service.

We believe the local church should be a welcoming home where people can hear God's Word, pray together, care for one another and serve with humility.

Our branch keeps things simple: know Christ, grow in faith and be a blessing to others.

BRANCH PASTOR

Pastor, Rukungiri Branch

Our local pastoral leader serving the Rukungiri church family.

OUR VALUES

A church for every family.

Christ-Centered

We keep Jesus at the center of our faith and daily life.

Bible-Based

We value God's Word as the foundation for how we live and serve.

Community-Minded

We seek to care for people and be a blessing to Rukungiri.
