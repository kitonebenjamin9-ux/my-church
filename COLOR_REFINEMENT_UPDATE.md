# HTC Colour Refinement Update

The previous rainbow-heavy visual treatment was replaced with a calmer church-focused palette:

- Deep Navy — trust and stability
- Royal Purple — ministry identity and dignity
- Gold — restrained accent and emphasis
- Ivory/White — clean backgrounds
- Teal — secondary accent used sparingly

The mobile menu links were reduced in height, padding and font size.
Member/Admin quick actions and dashboard/admin icons were also reduced for a cleaner layout.
Social media and WhatsApp retain recognisable app colours where appropriate.
