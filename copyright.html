Media & Copyright Policy

Guidance for church media, sermons, photographs and downloadable resources.

Church-produced media may be used for personal, non-commercial ministry purposes unless a page states otherwise.

Do not upload material you do not have permission to use. Administrators should verify permissions before publishing third-party photographs, music, video, documents or recordings.

If you believe material has been published in error, contact the church with the page, material and reason for the request.
