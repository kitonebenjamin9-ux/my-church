Thank You for Giving

Every Gift Helps Us Serve

We receive every gift with gratitude and steward it prayerfully in support of the ministry and its mission. You can give toward tithes, offerings, outreach, missions, youth mentorship and ministry projects.

Whether you are giving from Uganda or another part of the world, we want the process to be clear, secure and convenient.

The website currently does not have a connected international card/PayPal checkout, and the bank and mobile money details in the existing pages are placeholders.
