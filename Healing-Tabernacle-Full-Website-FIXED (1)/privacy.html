Privacy Policy

How Healing Tabernacle Ministries handles information submitted through this website.

We collect information you choose to submit, such as account details, event registrations, prayer requests, testimonies and contact messages.

We use this information to provide church services, respond to requests, manage registrations and communicate ministry updates.

We do not intentionally sell personal information to advertisers. Access to private information is limited by account roles and church administration needs.

You may contact the church to ask about your information or request appropriate correction. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate church, legal or accounting purposes.

Third-party services such as Firebase, video platforms, maps and payment providers may process information according to their own policies.
